Case study 01 · Marketplace · Vercel deployed · Pre-launch

KULTRAE | Building a culture-focused fashion marketplace

KULTRAE is a UK-first marketplace for buying, selling and renting cultural fashion. The product brings together product listings, seller profiles, account management, checkout and an administration experience.

Challenge and my role

Translate a multi-sided marketplace concept into usable flows while protecting user data and payment testing. I act as product owner and direct AI-assisted implementation, prioritisation, design review and acceptance checks.

What I worked on

  • Mapped shopper, seller and admin journeys, including listing and checkout requirements.
  • Used Codex with GitHub to scope changes, inspect fixes and review pull-request and CI outcomes.
  • Reviewed seller-profile navigation issues and tracked their fix through the development workflow.
  • Coordinated staging preparation with a visible test banner and restricted search indexing.

Technical approach

  • Next.js web frontend, Supabase database and authentication, GitHub and Vercel staging workflows.
  • Marketplace database migrations, row-level security and database index checks.
  • UK/GBP-specific marketplace rules and Stripe test-payment preparation.
  • Separated staging approval from production rollout to reduce deployment risk.

Documentation and learning

Turned feature goals into precise tasks, kept a record of release constraints, and distinguished CI success from completed end-to-end testing.

Current status: KULTRAE has been deployed to Vercel in a production environment, but has not launched for public use. Staging and database preparation have been tracked through development workflows. A deployment does not establish successful end-to-end checkout testing or a public launch.

Case study 02 · AI SaaS · In development

Sevorify | Designing an AI social content engine

Sevorify is a platform concept for helping clients turn their own photographs and videos into editable social content, supported by scheduling and publishing workflows.

Challenge and my role

Bring content creation, editing, approval and scheduling into a coherent experience rather than scattered tools. I lead product direction, requirements, design selection and AI-assisted iteration.

What I worked on

  • Outlined media upload, AI-generated content, editing, client workflows and publishing requirements.
  • Compared interface options and selected a dark navy experience with violet and pink accents.
  • Reviewed mobile responsiveness and raised non-clickable navigation links as a functional defect.
  • Planned a safe workflow to move the existing GitHub project from Claude Code-assisted development into Codex.

Technical approach

  • Existing GitHub codebase and iterative AI-assisted frontend development.
  • Responsive interface inspection with emphasis on mobile navigation and interaction.
  • Feature specifications and structured prompts to separate intended functionality from delivered functionality.
  • Incremental checks and version-controlled changes before publication.

Documentation and learning

Produced requirements and explained user journeys so that engineering tasks have a clear purpose and testable outcomes.

Current status: Design direction and requirements are defined, with the platform still under active development. Automatic posting, integrations and complete AI content workflows are product goals, not claimed shipped capabilities.
